# FUSE — 15-Day Poster Plan (Static / Carousel Track)

> **Standalone doc.** This is separate from `FUSE_MARKETING_PLAN.md` (the 75-reel blitz). It does not replace or modify the reel plan. Posters are the **static, screenshot-able track** that runs alongside the reels — one poster per day, 15 days, same 5-act arc so the two tracks reinforce each other on the same day.

## 0. Why posters, and why a separate track

Reels win attention. **Posters win the screenshot.** A reel is watched once and scrolls away; a poster is a still frame someone can screenshot, send to a friend on WhatsApp, or save. For a pre-launch dating/going-out app in one city, the WhatsApp forward *is* the distribution — a poster that lands as "haha this is so true" gets shared into exactly the group chats we want (21–30, Chennai, single-ish).

So posters are optimized differently from reels:

- **Stands alone with sound off and context zero.** No hook-retention curve to ride — the whole idea must land in one glance.
- **One idea per poster.** Reels can have a 5-beat arc; a poster has one line worth screenshotting.
- **Text-forward, meme-adjacent.** The line does the work; the design frames it.
- **Built to be saved and re-shared,** not just liked.

**Rule of thumb:** if you can't screenshot it and have it still make sense in a stranger's group chat, it's not a poster yet.

## 1. Design system (loose family, not a straitjacket)

Keep the grid feeling like one campaign — but through **tone and type discipline, not a forced color**. Variety is a feature: a mix of stark type posters, abstract art frames, and photo-real moments makes the feed look alive, not templated.

- **Color — a hint, not a rule.** Coral (`#ff5864` neighborhood) shows up as a *touch* — one word, a thin line, a glow, a single object — enough to whisper "Fuse." Posters can also be fully abstract or off-palette (moody film tones, warm Chennai golden-hour, high-contrast mono) when the idea is stronger without brand color. Never flood-fill coral; never let brand-matching flatten a good visual.
- **What holds it together instead of color:** consistent type voice (confident, plain-spoken), generous negative space, one idea per frame, and a recurring *small* `fuse` sting. The through-line is attitude, not a swatch.
- **Type:** Poppins (or close). Big, tight, confident. Statement in Bold/ExtraBold; source lines and captions in Regular. Type can *be* the whole poster.
- **Visual registers to rotate (keeps the grid textured):**
  - *Stark statement* — type on solid/gradient, near-zero imagery (Days 1, 2, 4, 6, 9).
  - *Abstract/conceptual* — metaphor as image (locked gift, two doors, algorithm-hand vs human-hand, the equation) — clean, editorial, a little surreal.
  - *Photo-real Chennai* — golden-hour, real rooms, run clubs, rooftops, a gift in hand — warm and human (Days 10, 11, 14).
  - *Data/receipt* — clinical charts and quotes with sources (Days 1, 3, 12).
- **Format mix:** ~10 single-frame + ~5 carousels (2–5 slides) for ideas that need a build.
- **Corner sting:** small `fuse` wordmark somewhere on every poster — understated pre-reveal (Days 1–6), confident after Day 7.
- **Safety protocol (carried from reel plan §4.5):** any competitor stat or claim shows its **source on-frame** (e.g. "Bloomberg, Q1 2026"), framed as opinion/fact — never fabricated. Attack the *system/model*, never a gender or a person's character.

> **For the prompt-gen step next:** every poster below now carries an *Art direction* line written to translate straight into an image prompt — subject, mood, composition, palette-freedom noted. We'll expand each into a full generation prompt one at a time.

## 2. The arc (poster track maps to the reel arc)

| Act | Days | Job | Poster feeling |
|-----|------|-----|----------------|
| **1 — Ignite** | 1–4 | Indict the category. Earn the "wait, true." | Provocative, factual, screenshot-bait |
| **2 — Story** | 5–7 | Name the pain nobody's naming. Soft "something's coming." | Human, relatable, a little raw |
| **3 — Reveal** | 8–10 | Introduce Fuse: the equation + two doors + the one rule | Confident, clean, "oh — *that's* the idea" |
| **4 — Proof** | 11–13 | How we build + why to trust it + who's already here | Transparent, warm, real |
| **5 — Pull** | 14–15 | Founding 100 call + full-circle callback to Day 1 | Urgent, invitational, closed loop |

Day-alignment anchors kept in sync with the reel plan: **Day 6** soft name-drop, **Day 7** reveal, **Day 13** Founding-100 launch, **Day 15** full-circle callback to Day 1.

## 3. The 15 posters

Each spec: **Hook** (the screenshot line) · **Visual** · **Format** · **Caption direction** · **Pillar** · **Founder rationale**. On-screen copy is draft — Viggy localizes to Tanglish where it should bite.

### ACT 1 — IGNITE

---

**Day 1 — "Swiping is dead."**
- **Hook (on-frame):** `SWIPING IS DEAD.` — subline: *We have the numbers.*
- **Visual:** Black frame, coral punch word, three source stats stacked small at the bottom.
- **Format:** Single frame (optionally 3-slide carousel: claim → 3 stats → "Chennai, something's coming").
- **Source stats on-frame:** Bumble paying users −21% (Q1 2026), Tinder MAU −7%, 79% of Gen Z report dating-app fatigue. Each with source tag.
- **Caption:** The category everyone still uses is quietly collapsing. Here's the receipts. — no product mention yet.
- **Pillar:** Controversy / category indictment.
- **Rationale:** Day 1 must be a slap that's *defensible*. Sources on-frame = shareable without being dismissed as a hot take. Sets the whole 15-day tension: if swiping's dead, what's next? (Callback target for Day 15.)

---

**Day 2 — "Their profit is your loneliness."**
- **Hook:** `The app makes money when you DON'T find someone.`
- **Visual:** Simple business-model diagram — a heart on a subscription meter that only pays out while you're still single.
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** Every incumbent's revenue line goes up the longer you stay single and swiping. The incentive is the problem. Whose side is your dating app on?
- **Pillar:** Controversy / incentive misalignment.
- **Rationale:** Moves from "it's dying" to "it was rigged." This is the most WhatsApp-forwardable idea in the set — it reframes a private frustration as a system critique. No Fuse yet; still building the problem.

---

**Day 3 — "The ratio is the business."**
- **Hook:** `Roughly 70% of dating-app users are men. That's not a bug — it's the model.`
- **Visual:** A stark 70/30 bar. Neutral, clinical. Not a jab at either gender — a jab at the system that profits from the imbalance.
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** When one side massively outnumbers the other, the experience breaks for everyone — men shout into the void, women get buried. Attack the math, not the people. There's a better shape for this.
- **Pillar:** Controversy / structural.
- **Rationale:** The ratio is the deepest, least-said truth in dating apps. Framing it as "the business" (not "women should…") keeps it safe and smart. Seeds the two-door female-first design without revealing it.

---

**Day 4 — "Designed to be deleted?"**
- **Hook:** `"Designed to be deleted." — then why does it want you swiping forever?`
- **Visual:** The famous slogan in quotes, struck through, coral. Ironic, clean.
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** The slogan is beautiful. The endless-swipe UX says otherwise. A promise the product can't afford to keep.
- **Pillar:** Controversy / competitor irony (Hinge).
- **Rationale:** Weaponizes a competitor's own tagline — high share-value, low risk (it's their words, our observation). Closes Act 1 on the sharpest incumbent-specific note before we pivot from villain to human pain.

---

### ACT 2 — STORY

---

**Day 5 — "The parts they don't put in the ad."**
- **Hook (cover slide):** `Nobody warns you about the parts AFTER the match.`
- **Visual:** 5-slide carousel, one pain per slide, plain type: the talking stage that goes nowhere · the paywall on basic features · getting ghosted post-date · matching with someone across the city you'll never actually meet · doing all this *alone*.
- **Format:** Carousel (5 slides).
- **Caption:** The match was never the hard part. Which one got you?
- **Pillar:** Pain / relatability.
- **Rationale:** First fully human poster — invites replies ("all of them 💀"). Each slide is independently screenshot-able. The last slide ("alone") quietly sets up the "never go alone" reveal without saying the product name.

---

**Day 6 — "Nobody's proud they met on an app."**
- **Hook:** `Be honest — when people ask how you met, you lower your voice.`
- **Visual:** Muted photo-style frame, the line large. Small coral `fuse` sting appears cleaner here — first soft signal.
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** The meet-cute gap: we all want the story, none of us want the origin. What if the app got you *out of the app*? Something's coming to Chennai.
- **Pillar:** Pain / aspiration (soft name-drop).
- **Rationale:** Day 6 is the reel plan's soft name-drop day — poster matches. Names the emotional gap Fuse fills (offline, real-world) and plants "something's coming" for the Day 7 reveal.

---

**Day 7 — REVEAL: the equation.**
- **Hook:** `Hinge + Blinkit + District = Fuse`
- **Visual:** The core visual motif animated for reels, here as a clean static lockup: match + instant gifting + events → one coral Fuse mark. This is the campaign's signature frame.
- **Format:** Single frame (hero) + optional 3-slide breakdown (what each term means).
- **Caption:** Match someone. Send a real gift after a real conversation. Go out — with your match, or with the city. One app. Chennai-built. This is Fuse.
- **Pillar:** Reveal / positioning.
- **Rationale:** The payoff of 6 days of tension. The equation is the most memorable single asset in the whole campaign — it's a sticker, a T-shirt, a sting. Everything before points here; everything after explains it.

---

### ACT 3 — REVEAL (continued)

---

**Day 8 — "Two doors."**
- **Hook:** `You don't have to be looking for a date to open this app.`
- **Visual:** Two-door split frame — **Meet People** (run clubs, house parties, open mics) | **Date Ideas** (matches + gifting). One product, two ways in.
- **Format:** Carousel (cover → Meet People → Date Ideas → "come for one, stay for both").
- **Caption:** Come for the run club. Stay for the person you met there. Fuse is the going-out app where you never go alone.
- **Pillar:** Feature / positioning.
- **Rationale:** Kills the #1 objection ("I'm not on dating apps") by making the events door the front door. This is also the female-first design in disguise — lower-stakes entry. Directly supports the events-led acquisition engine.

---

**Day 9 — "You can't buy your way in."**
- **Hook:** `You can only send a gift AFTER a real conversation. On purpose.`
- **Visual:** A gift box behind a soft lock; the key is a chat bubble. Coral accent on "AFTER."
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** Gifts on Fuse can't buy attention, a reply, or a date. They're a gesture you unlock by actually talking to someone. Safety isn't a feature we shout — it's how the whole thing is built.
- **Pillar:** Feature / values (safety, quietly).
- **Rationale:** Turns the gift *rule* into the flex. Differentiates hard from "sugar/cash" perceptions and states the values without preaching. Safety stays quiet-but-present, per positioning.

---

**Day 10 — "Never go alone."**
- **Hook:** `See who else is going — before you go.`
- **Visual:** An Event Circle mock — attendee grid (blurred/stylized), a "wave" tap. "3 people you'd vibe with are going Saturday."
- **Format:** Carousel (event → circle → wave → match).
- **Caption:** RSVP, see who's in the room, wave first. Event Circles turn "I don't want to go alone" into "I already know three people there."
- **Pillar:** Feature / social proof engine.
- **Rationale:** Shows the retention/acquisition loop that makes cold-start believable — an event with a few Fuse people looks full from day one. Most concrete "what you actually get" poster.

---

### ACT 4 — PROOF

---

**Day 11 — "Built in Chennai, in public."**
- **Hook:** `We're building this in the open. Here's where we are: Day __, __ real users, one city.`
- **Visual:** Founder-transparent style, handwritten-feel numbers, coral underline. Warm, honest, not polished-corporate.
- **Format:** Single frame (updatable number).
- **Caption:** No fake reviews, no bought followers, no other city yet. Just Chennai and the Founding 100. You're early — that's the point.
- **Pillar:** Meta / build-in-public trust.
- **Rationale:** Transparency is the trust unlock for an unknown app. "Early" reframes small numbers as exclusivity, not weakness. Founder-voice earns the follow.

---

**Day 12 — "AI can't feel who you love."**
- **Hook:** `A connection you didn't choose isn't a match. It's an assignment.`
- **Visual:** A cold algorithm-hand vs a human hand reaching back. Coral only on "you didn't choose."
- **Format:** Single frame.
- **Caption:** The new wave of AI dating apps wants to pick for you. We think that's backwards. Fuse is a platform, not a puppeteer — we put you in real rooms and let *you* choose. (On Overtone & the AI-dating trend — sourced.)
- **Pillar:** Controversy / philosophy (proof-of-values).
- **Rationale:** [viggy1] concept in poster form. Timely (AI dating is in the press), stakes out Fuse's soul, and gives the campaign a second sharp-take beat inside the proof act. Source-cited to stay safe.

---

**Day 13 — FOUNDING 100 LAUNCH.**
- **Hook:** `The first 100 people in Chennai get to shape this. 100 only.`
- **Visual:** A clean counter/badge — "Founding 100 · Chennai · [__] spots left." Coral. Scarcity, not hype.
- **Format:** Carousel (the invite → what you get → how to join → who it's for).
- **Caption:** Founding members get first access, a direct line to the founder, and the story of "I was here first." Link in bio. Chennai only.
- **Pillar:** Launch / CTA.
- **Rationale:** The commercial peak — matches the reel plan's Day 13 launch. Everything converts here. Scarcity is real (density strategy), so it's honest, not manufactured.

---

### ACT 5 — PULL

---

**Day 14 — "This weekend on Fuse."**
- **Hook:** `This is what "going out" looked like on Fuse this week.`
- **Visual:** A roundup grid — events that happened / are happening, tagged organizers, a couple of (consented/stylized) moments. Real, warm, alive.
- **Format:** Carousel (weekend roundup).
- **Caption:** Run club Saturday. House party Sunday. A few first hellos we're not going to spoil. Tag who you'd bring. — organizer tags for cross-promo reach.
- **Pillar:** Proof / FOMO / organizer channel.
- **Rationale:** Social proof that the thing is *live*, not vapor. Activates the ₹0-CAC organizer channel via tags. Turns "should I join?" into "I'm missing this."

---

**Day 15 — FULL CIRCLE: "Swiping is dead. This is what's next."**
- **Hook:** `Day 1 we said swiping is dead. Day 15 — here's what replaces it.`
- **Visual:** Direct callback to Day 1's frame (same layout, same coral), but the "dead" struck through and the Fuse equation / two-door mark completing it. The loop closes on screen.
- **Format:** Single frame (or 2-slide: Day 1 frame → Day 15 answer).
- **Caption:** Not another app to swipe on. A way to actually go out, in your city, without going alone. Chennai — you're early. Founding 100 open. — final CTA.
- **Pillar:** Pull / callback / close.
- **Rationale:** Payoff of the whole arc. Rewards everyone who followed from Day 1, converts fence-sitters with a resolved narrative, and leaves the grid as a self-contained story someone can scroll top-to-bottom. Highest reason-to-follow-and-share of the set.

## 4b. Niche-Idea Bank (draw-from pool)

Extra concepts rooted specifically in **Fuse's niche** — the going-out app where you never go alone, offline meet-cutes, gifting-after-conversation, event circles, match→real-plan, Chennai locality. Use these to **swap into a day, extend the campaign past 15, or spin B-side posters.** Each: hook + art-direction seed + niche it depicts.

- **"The app that wants you to leave it."** — A door left open, phone face-down on the table, city glowing outside. *Niche: get-off-the-app / offline-first (the honest inverse of "designed to be deleted").*
- **"Match on Tuesday. Actually meet on Saturday."** — Split calendar: a chat bubble on one side, a real cafe table on the other, an arrow between. *Niche: match → real plan, closing the meet-cute gap.*
- **"The talking stage has a graveyard. We skip it."** — A field of dead-grey chat bubbles as headstones; one bright path leads out to a real event. *Niche: anti-endless-texting, push to real-world.*
- **"You don't need a date to belong here."** — A warm group in a room, one newcomer welcomed; no romance framing at all. *Niche: Meet People door / low-stakes female-first entry.*
- **"Wave first. Talk later. No pressure."** — A hand doing a small wave across a room of stylized attendees. *Niche: Event Circle wave mechanic, consent-forward.*
- **"A gift should mean something. So we made it earn its way."** — A single thoughtful object handed over after a visible conversation thread. *Niche: gifting economy tied to real interaction, not payment-for-attention.*
- **"Chennai has 200 things to do this weekend. Now you have someone to do them with."** — A city map lit with event pins, two figures meeting at one. *Niche: local events aggregation + never-go-alone, hyperlocal.*
- **"Verified room. Real people. No bots."** — A house-party scene with a subtle verified check on the door. *Niche: safety-quietly + real-world trust.*

- **"Come for the run club. Leave with a story."** — Runners at dawn on the Marina/ECR, two glancing at each other mid-stride. *Niche: event-led acquisition, the run-club hero image.*
- **"Your dating app is lonely. On purpose. Ours puts you in a room."** — One person alone in a phone-glow vs a lit room full of people. *Niche: loneliness critique → group discovery answer.*
