## 0. The one-paragraph brief (what this reel is)

The reel is built on one spine line, spoken as the **hook**: **"Systems that profit while pretending to serve you. People manipulated by the very thing that's supposed to help them. Your rights as a user."** The cultural mood right now — people alert to being manipulated by systems meant to help them — is **evoked indirectly**: we open *universal* (so anyone angry at any "system that was supposed to serve you" feels seen), let the current moment resonate purely by **adjacency + posting timing**, then **narrow to the one system we're actually qualified to indict: the dating app.** Viggy — a dating-app founder — turns whistleblower on his own industry, shows how dating apps are *engineered* to keep you single, lonely, and paying, and lands on: *"So I built the opposite."*

**The structure = universal → indirect nod → our niche → Fuse.** Start broad and defiant (everyone nods), gesture at the mood without ever naming it, zoom into dating apps, close on Fuse (offline-first, "the app that wants you to leave it," Chennai, Founding 100).

**Why it goes viral:** the manifesto-cadence cold open is a hard thumb-stop; it reframes a private frustration everyone feels as a *system run against them* (high forward-value); it's a founder betraying his own industry's secrets (insider credibility + spice); and it ends on a product that is literally the resolution.

**Hard guardrail — the "indirect" MUST stay indirect:** never name the event, cause, or movement; no protest slogans, imagery, hashtags, dates, or names; no reference to any tragedy, exam, or students-by-name. The nod is created by a *universal* opening + *timing*, never by pointing at the specific event. Attack the **system / business model**, never a gender, a person, or anyone's pain. (Carries §4.5 safety protocol; see [[feedback_borrow_mood_not_cause]].) **The test:** if someone personally affected by the current moment saw this, it must read as "a founder calling out his own industry," never as "a brand standing on our pain."

## 1. Format decision — founder-to-camera + background motion graphics

**Recommended: Viggy on camera, with animated motion-graphics behind/around him** (the slot machine, the meter, the swipe-tunnel appear as overlays as he names them).

Why this beats full AI video for *this* reel:
- A manipulation exposé lives or dies on **trust**. "I run a dating app and here's the dirty secret" only lands from a **real human face** saying it to you. AI video reads as an ad; a founder confession reads as truth.
- Motion graphics do the *illustrating* (slot machine, paywall, loneliness-meter) so Viggy's words stay punchy while the eye gets fed — best of both, cheap to make.
- It's repeatable: this becomes Viggy's on-camera "founder truth-bomb" format he can reuse for a whole series.

**Faceless / AI-video fallback** (for a second variant to A/B for reach) is specced in §7 — same script, no face, pure kinetic-type + motion graphics + voiceover. Post both, keep whichever the algorithm rewards.

Length target: **45–50s** (the manifesto cold open earns a little more room). Fast cuts, no dead air.

## 2. The hook (locked — this is the spine line)

**The hook is the triad, delivered as three hard punches to camera over the first ~6 seconds** — stark, placard-energy, high-contrast bold type flashing with each line (borrow the *look* of principled defiance; never any protest imagery):

> **"Systems that profit while pretending to serve you.**
> **People manipulated by the very thing that's supposed to help them.**
> **And nobody talks about your rights — as a user."**

Delivery: near-quiet, controlled, almost angry — a manifesto, not a rant. Each sentence is its own on-screen caption card. The word **"user"** is the pivot — it's the first hint this is about an *app*, not the thing the viewer was thinking about.

**Two optional alternate openers to shoot for A/B (the triad stays the spine either way):**
- **Tighter cold-open (for pure reach):** first frame is just the caption `Systems that profit while pretending to serve you.` on black, then Viggy picks up the other two lines. Fastest thumb-stop.

## 9. Why this is safe *and* sharp — and the "indirect nod" dial

- **Safe:** the villain is a **business model**, not a person, a gender, or anyone's pain. Nothing on screen references a cause, event, exam, or tragedy. If a stranger — or someone personally affected by the current moment — screenshots it, it reads as "a founder calling out his *own* industry," principled, not exploitative.
- **Sharp:** it opens on a universal nerve everyone privately feels, then says the thing nobody in the dating category will admit — and the person saying it has standing (he builds these). Manifesto energy + insider betrayal = the viral engine.
- **On-brand:** it's Day 2 ("their profit is your loneliness") with a face + the "leave the app" / offline-first niche. Perfectly consistent with the campaign spine.

**The indirect-nod dial (how "indirect" to go):** the adjacency to the current moment should come almost entirely from **posting timing** (publish while the mood is hot) — *not* from on-screen pointing. The one soft textual nod is the word **"teach you,"** buried as one item in the universal list at [0:06–0:12]. Three settings:
- **Default (recommended):** keep "teach you" in the list of five — universal, deniable, tasteful.
- **Softer:** drop "teach you," use only "help you / hire you / keep you safe / connect you." Adjacency then rides 100% on timing. Use this if the moment is especially raw.
- **Never:** do **not** add exams, students, coaching, marks, a specific institution, or any word that singles out the education angle. That crosses from *evoke* to *appropriate* and fails the test. When in doubt, go softer.
